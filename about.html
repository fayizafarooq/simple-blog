My name is Isabelle Perez and this is how simple living changed my life

One of the hardest lessons was learning to let go of comparison. I used to measure my worth against others, friends who seemed ahead in their careers, people who looked happier online, or those who had what I thought I lacked. Choosing simplicity meant choosing peace. I stopped chasing timelines that weren’t mine and started appreciating the small, everyday wins. That shift gave me freedom to breathe, to grow, and to enjoy the present moment.

A Life That Feels Whole

Today, simplicity shapes everything I do. From the way I work, to how I decorate my home, to the food I cook, it’s all about balance, clarity, and joy. I’ve learned that life doesn’t have to be complicated to be meaningful. By focusing on routines that ground me, relationships that uplift me, and goals that align with my values, I’ve built a life that feels whole. And that, to me, is the true gift of living simply.

Embracing Simplicity

For me, simple living isn’t about doing less, it’s about living with more intention. When you let go of comparison and focus on what truly matters, life becomes lighter, clearer, and far more meaningful. My journey has taught me that simplicity is not just a choice, it’s a way of life.

Simplicity gave me back my peace and with it I found myself.
